Subject: KNEST platform: documents, approvals and information required from KIIT before public launch

Dear KNEST Team,

Thank you for your continued collaboration on the KNEST platform. The application, content, review and laboratory modules are built and have passed our automated testing. Before the platform can be made public and begin collecting real student and applicant data, a number of legal documents, approvals and institutional facts are needed from KIIT. These are institutional decisions that we cannot author or approve on KIIT's behalf, and the platform will continue to show an honest "being finalised" notice on its privacy and terms pages until they are in place.

We would be grateful if you could provide the items below, or name the person responsible for each, and indicate a target date. A response table is included at the end for convenience.

1. LEGAL DOCUMENTS

1.1 Privacy Notice. The final, approved text, covering who we are, what is collected and why, who can see it, where it is stored, how long it is kept, user rights and how to exercise them, treatment of minors, cookies, and how changes are announced.

1.2 Terms and Conditions of Use. The final, approved text, covering eligibility and accounts, applications, ownership and licence of submitted content, acceptable use, treatment of accounts at graduation, laboratory booking rules, governing law and jurisdiction, and changes to the terms.

1.3 Consent wording. The exact text of the sign-up consent statement, and confirmation of whether separate consent is required for photographs, mentor profiles in the public directory, and testimonials.

1.4 Cookie statement. Confirmation that the planned use (one sign-in cookie and one anonymous analytics cookie, with no advertising or third-party tracking) is acceptable to KIIT.

2. DATA PROTECTION DECISIONS

2.1 The legal entity that acts as the data fiduciary, and the name, designation and contact details of the privacy and grievance officer to be published.

2.2 KIIT's position on the applicability of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and any existing KIIT privacy policy that the notice should adopt or align with.

2.3 Treatment of users under 18: whether verifiable parental consent is required, or whether enrolled students are treated as adults for this purpose.

2.4 Retention periods for: accepted and rejected applications, uploaded documents, accounts that are never verified, analytics records, security logs, and laboratory attendance records.

2.5 The process for access, correction and deletion requests: who receives them, the response time, and who may carry out a deletion.

2.6 Any sharing of applicant data outside the KNEST staff (mentors, partners, investors, other KIIT units), and the basis for it.

2.7 Cross-border processing: confirmation that hosting in India (AWS Mumbai) with a global anti-abuse service (Cloudflare Turnstile) is acceptable.

2.8 Language: English only, or also Hindi and Odia.

3. CONTENT AND PUBLIC STATEMENTS

3.1 Written approval of the public copy for the Home, About, Ecosystem and Invest pages, and confirmation of any statistics, rankings or claims that may be published.

3.2 The name of the person authorised to approve content and to sign the approval record, together with the date of approval.

3.3 Real programme information (descriptions, eligibility, deadlines, application questions), mentor and startup profiles, events and resources, with written permission from each person or organisation named.

3.4 Photographs and logos that KIIT owns or is licensed to use, with the licence or permission on record.

4. LABORATORY OPERATIONS

4.1 Laboratory policy values: slot length, advance-booking horizon, minimum notice, cancellation cut-off, limits per person and per week, who is eligible, and handling of no-shows and damage.

4.2 The list of laboratories, their departments and hierarchy, operating hours, closed dates, and the head and assistants for each.

4.3 Review of the laboratory attendance design (signed QR passes, offline operation) by a laboratory head, and a decision on whether the QR pass records attendance only or controls physical access.

5. ACCOUNTS, DOMAIN AND OPERATIONS

5.1 Written confirmation of the owner of the domain kiitnest.com (and kiitnest.in, if used) and of the hosting account, and confirmation that these are held by KIIT or KNEST in an institutional account.

5.2 An institutional mailbox for billing and alert notices, and the name of the person responsible for the cloud bill.

5.3 The support contact and response expectations for urgent incidents, and the mailbox that should receive contact-form enquiries and system mail.

5.4 The sender address to be used for platform email, and authority to add the required records to the domain's DNS.

5.5 Authority to open the required service accounts in KIIT's name (email delivery and bot protection), or the named person who will do so.

6. COMMERCIAL AND CONTRACTUAL

6.1 A copy of the agreement, invoice and scope document that KIIT holds, so that delivery can be reconciled against exactly what was agreed.

6.2 Confirmation of whether the QR pass, installable app and offline operation modules are required for the first release or may follow it.

7. ACCEPTANCE AND TRAINING

7.1 Named staff to carry out acceptance testing of the review and content workflows.

7.2 Named participants for training sessions (KNEST staff and laboratory heads).

7.3 A named approver and date for the final go-live decision.

A NOTE ON ROLES

The engineering team will implement the approved text exactly as supplied and will not draft, approve or sign legal or policy documents on KIIT's behalf. Where helpful, a draft outline of the privacy notice and terms, prepared from what the system actually does, is available for your legal team to correct and adopt. Nothing in it is legal advice.

WHAT HAPPENS WITHOUT THESE ITEMS

The platform can be tested internally and shown in a restricted preview. It will not be opened to the public, and it will not collect real student data, until the privacy notice, terms and content approvals above are on record.
