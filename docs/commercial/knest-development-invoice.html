Why the price is what it is

A price this far below market invites a fair question. The honest answer, in four parts:

  • It is built by a KIIT student, for KIIT. The rate is a student rate, not a market rate. That is a deliberate concession to the institution, not a reflection of the work's value.
  • KNEST is the reference deployment. Being able to point at a live platform serving a real university is part of the consideration. That has value to the supplier, and it is priced in.
  • There is no agency overhead. No sales team, no account managers, no office, no margin stacked on subcontracted work. Agency rates carry all four; this does not.
  • The estimate risk sits with the supplier. This is a fixed price against a fixed scope. If a module takes twice the estimated hours, that is absorbed, not invoiced. Agencies price that risk in; time-and-materials contracts pass it to the client.

  • The source code. Held in KNEST's own repository, with no supplier claim over it.
  • The cloud accounts. Every AWS and Cloudflare resource sits in KNEST-owned accounts, not the supplier's.
  • The data. Exportable in full, at any time, in open formats.
  • No per-seat licence, ever. Unlimited students, founders, mentors, lab heads and admins at no additional cost.
  • No lock-in. Standard Next.js, PostgreSQL and AWS. Any competent developer can take it over — the maintenance contract is a choice, not a dependency.
  • No renewal cliff. Nothing stops working if a contract lapses.

This matters more than it first appears. Per-seat platforms in this category charge by the student, every year, forever, and hold the data. Here, growth from a hundred users to thirty thousand costs KNEST nothing in licensing.
