Why this contract exists

What is live is not one website. It is a public platform holding real applicant documents, and a booking system that decides who gets physical access to KIIT labs — running across eight AWS services and Cloudflare. Software of that shape does not stand still. Security advisories are published against its dependencies, AWS deprecates runtimes, Android and iOS releases break camera and offline behaviour in progressive web apps, intake cycles bring new programmes and new questions, new labs come online each term, and databases need looking after.

This contract covers all of it at a fixed annual fee, so KNEST is not negotiating a price every time something needs attention — and so there is a named person accountable when a student cannot get into a lab at nine in the morning.

Why this is not a percentage of the build price

Maintenance contracts are often quoted as 15–40% of what the build cost. That convention only works when the build was priced at market. This one was not: it was written by a KIIT student for KIIT, at a flat ₹200 an hour against a market band of ₹1,000–₹1,800. Taking a percentage of an already-conceded price would set the maintenance fee by an accident of who built it, rather than by the work involved.

So this fee is built the way the invoice was — from hours. 480 hours across the year, itemised below, at the same flat ₹200 an hour. Nothing is derived from the build price, and the concession granted there is not quietly recovered here: the same rate applies to both.

What it costs not to have this. One intake window missed because sign-in broke, one morning of founders turned away from a lab because QR scanning failed, or one applicant's uploaded documents exposed — any of the three costs KNEST more, in reputation and in scramble, than the annual fee. Maintenance is not insurance against unlikely events; it is the work that stops likely ones.

Outside this contract

Charged separately

  • Enhancement work beyond the included 24 hours a year, at ₹700 per hour, estimated and approved in writing before work starts.
  • New modules or significant features — quoted per module, on the same basis as the development invoice.
  • Visual redesign or rebranding beyond the existing design system.
  • Migration off AWS Amplify or RDS to a different provider or architecture.
  • Scanner hardware, tablets or printed QR signage for lab desks.
  • Content writing, photography, data entry and bulk data imports.

Not covered

  • AWS and Cloudflare service charges, billed by those providers directly to KNEST.
  • Domain registration and renewal, which KNEST holds and pays for directly.
  • Faults caused by changes made to the code or infrastructure by anyone other than the supplier.
  • Loss or corruption caused by KNEST staff actions in the admin console — recovery is chargeable at the hourly rate above.
  • Outages attributable to AWS, Cloudflare or another provider, beyond triage, escalation and reporting.

Terms

  • Term. Twelve months from 01 October 2026, renewable by mutual consent. Any renewal increase is capped at 10% of the then-current fee.
  • Termination. Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice. On termination, fees for the unexpired period are refunded pro rata, less any enhancement work already delivered.
  • Handover on exit. All source code, credentials, documentation and data export are handed over within 14 days of termination, at no charge.
  • Ownership. KNEST owns the source code and all data. The supplier retains no rights over either, and may reference the project as portfolio work unless KNEST asks otherwise in writing.
  • Confidentiality. Applicant and member data is confidential, accessed only where necessary to deliver support, and never copied out of the production environment except for a documented, secured backup restore.
  • Continuity. If the supplier is unable to continue, an orderly handover of at least 30 days is provided, including documentation and a working session with any incoming team.
  • Liability. The supplier's aggregate liability under this contract is limited to the annual fee paid.
  • Escalation. Anything unresolved past its target resolution time is escalated to the named KNEST point of contact with a written explanation and a revised commitment.
