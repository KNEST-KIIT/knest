Why this contract exists

The platform is live, holds real applicant data and is the public face of KNEST. Software of this kind does not stand still: browsers change, security advisories are published against the libraries it depends on, intake cycles bring new programmes and new questions, and databases need looking after. This contract covers that ongoing work at a fixed annual fee, so KNEST is not negotiating a price every time something needs attention.

The fee is 39% of the development cost — within the customary 15–40% band for annual maintenance on a platform of this size.

Outside this contract

Charged separately

  • Enhancement work beyond the included 12 hours a year, at ₹700 per hour, estimated and approved in writing before work starts.
  • New modules or significant features — quoted per module, on the same basis as the development invoice.
  • Visual redesign or rebranding beyond the existing design system.
  • Migration to a different hosting provider or platform.
  • Content writing, photography, data entry and bulk data imports.

Not covered

  • Third-party service fees and licences — hosting, database, storage, email.
  • Domain registration and renewal, which KNEST holds and pays for directly.
  • Faults caused by changes made to the code or infrastructure by anyone other than the supplier.
  • Loss or corruption caused by KNEST staff actions in the admin console — recovery is chargeable at the hourly rate above.
  • Outages attributable to a third-party provider, beyond triage, escalation and reporting.

Terms

  • Term. Twelve months from 01 October 2026, renewable by mutual consent. Any renewal increase is capped at 10% of the then-current fee.
  • Termination. Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice. On termination, fees for the unexpired period are refunded pro rata, less any enhancement work already delivered.
  • Handover on exit. All source code, credentials, documentation and data export are handed over within 14 days of termination, at no charge.
  • Ownership. KNEST owns the source code and all data. The supplier retains no rights over either, and may reference the project as portfolio work unless KNEST asks otherwise in writing.
  • Confidentiality. Applicant and member data is confidential, accessed only where necessary to deliver support, and never copied out of the production environment except for a documented, secured backup restore.
  • Continuity. If the supplier is unable to continue, an orderly handover of at least 30 days is provided, including documentation and a working session with any incoming team.
  • Liability. The supplier's aggregate liability under this contract is limited to the annual fee paid.
  • Escalation. Anything unresolved past its target resolution time is escalated to the named KNEST point of contact with a written explanation and a revised commitment.
